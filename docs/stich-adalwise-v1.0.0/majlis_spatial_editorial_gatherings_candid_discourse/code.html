What to Expect at a Majlis

روایات اور آدابِ مجلس
01

A Home Setting

Held in Dr. Haseeb's personal living study in Model Town. Relaxed floor cushions, sheer afternoon daylight filtering through Lahore courtyards, shoes off at the entrance. The domestic space dismantles academic formality.

02

Chai & Home Lunch

Freshly brewed cardamom and ginger doodh patti served immediately upon arrival. Deliberations pause at 1:30 PM for a traditional sit-down, home-cooked communal lunch, where conversations deepen across the table.

03

Candid Inquiry

No spectator rows or polite monologues. Every seat is expected to probe arguments, test assumptions, and cite primary sources with rigor and humility.

04

Four-Hour Rhythm

From 11:30 AM to 3:30 PM every alternate Sunday. Unhurried cadence allows ideas to breathe, debate to resolve, and fellowship to take root.

Reserving Your Presence

Physical seats are strictly limited to 14 participants to keep conversation organic. If you wish to join the next circle, kindly connect via WhatsApp.
