## Divergence as Intellectual Vitality

In classical jurisprudence, divergence of reasoned opinion (*Ikhtilaf*) among qualified jurists was recognized as a manifestation of mercy and systemic adaptability—distinctly separated from sectarian rupture (*Iftiraq*).

> **Axiom of Imam Abu Hanifah:**
> *"This which we hold is our opinion, yet it is the best we could reach. Whoever arrives with a sounder argument, theirs is more entitled to adoption."*
