## The Semantic Integrity of Public Speech

When words lose their precise referents, society slides into epistemic chaos. In classical scholarship, *Ilm al-Balaghat* was never an art of deception or cosmetic ornamentation; it was the discipline of matching speech to the requirements of objective reality (*Muqabalah li Muqtada al-Hal*).

Modern political speech routinely subverts this by deploying emotional hyperbole to mask structural failure. Classical Usul demands semantic precision before ethical evaluation.
