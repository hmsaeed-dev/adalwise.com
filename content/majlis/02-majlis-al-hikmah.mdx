Convened in the library of Bait al-Hikmah in Model Town, Lahore. The gathering examined historical precedents of classical juristic constraints upon administrative state excess, the role of equity (*Istihsan*), and reforming contemporary legal pedagogy.

## Juristic Deliberation

The third Majlis concentrated on the procedural boundaries of *Ijtihad* when confronted with the expanding bureaucratic powers of the modern nation-state. Key questions tackled:

* Can substantive justice (*Adl*) be preserved when judicial review is subordinated to executive statute?
* Classical benchmarks set by early judges (*Qudat*) in nullifying confiscatory state taxation.
* Constructing an authentic syllabus bridging classical Usul al-Fiqh with modern common law jurisprudential standards.
