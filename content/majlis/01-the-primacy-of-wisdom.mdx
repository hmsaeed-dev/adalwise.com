The inaugural gathering of the Adlwise Majlis, addressing the structural rupture introduced by colonial legal codification and the recovery of organic epistemological traditions in Islamic jurisprudence.

## Initial Deliberations

Gathered at the National Heritage Library in Islamabad, fellows and scholars interrogated the legacy of Anglo-Muhammadan law, tracing how codification stripped classical legal thought of its dynamic, pluralistic adjudicative discretion.
