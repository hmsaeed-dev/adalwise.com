A focused gathering exploring the statecraft of Madinah, the Mithaq al-Madina as a foundational constitutional covenant, and the structural application of the Prophetic revolutionary methodology (*Manhaj-e-Inqilab-e-Nabawi ﷺ*) in modern Pakistan.

## Thematic Premise

Modern constitutional discourse in Pakistan frequently finds itself trapped between secular proceduralism and autocratic codifications of faith. This session returns to the earliest primary political instrument of the Islamic polity—the Charter of Medina—to examine:

1. **Covenantal Federalism:** How multi-confessional civic treaties protect communal spheres while binding all parties into a collective civic peace.
2. **Prophetic Statecraft:** The transition from moral pedagogy to institutional governance without dissolving individual liberties.
3. **Public Ethics:** The institutional reactivation of *Hisbah* and civic accountability within contemporary administrative frameworks.
