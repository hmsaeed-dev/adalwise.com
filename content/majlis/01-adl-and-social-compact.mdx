A deliberation on the doctrine of *Maslahah Mursalah* (unrestricted public interest) and its dialectic with textual determinism in modern legislative assemblies.

## Topics Explored

The dialogue addressed the delicate equilibrium between codified law and organic customary protections (*'Urf*). Participants considered how covenantal fidelity in early Islamic statecraft informs ethical limitations on executive emergency powers.
